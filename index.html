A 150-Year-Old Movement, Re-Ignited

“The evangelization of the world
in this generation.”

A hundred and fifty years ago, a handful of college students gathered to pray, and a fire started. It became the Student Volunteer Movement — and over the next forty years, more than 20,000 young people left their campuses and went to the nations. One move of God among students changed the course of the world. Then the fire faded. Today, we are gathering a new generation to light it again — and SVM Mission Conference 2026 is where it begins.

Before You Come

Questions, Answered

What is SVM?

The Student Volunteer Movement — an independent, campus-based mission movement that exists to do one thing: raise up this generation of students and send them to the nations. It's not a club or a single church's program; local churches and the IM Mission network stand behind it together, so that any student who says “I'll go” has a real, open door in front of them.

Who is the conference for?

Students and young adults — from high schoolers to young professionals — and the leaders who invest in them. If the nations stir something in you, this night is for you.

How much does it cost?

The conference is free to attend. For the mission trips that follow, you cover your flight — housing and on-field costs are covered, with no agency fees.

What will I actually do on a trip?

You'll teach English through daily conversation sessions, serve alongside local churches and missionaries, share the gospel and lead Bible-based lessons, and build relationships with local students — while being discipled yourself. Trips run during school breaks (December, January, and May–August), and you can stay anywhere from a week to a full year.

Do I have to commit to going overseas?

No. Come to worship, walk the tent village, and simply see. Take a next step only if — and when — you're ready.

Where exactly will it be held?

In Bryan–College Station, Texas. The exact venue is being finalized and will be announced soon — register and we'll email you the details first.

Who is hosting it?

IM Mission Foundation, together with co-hosting churches and a committee of regional leaders. SVM is an independent movement the churches support — not a single church's event.
